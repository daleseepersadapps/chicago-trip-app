# Photo credits

Every tile on the globe is prefilled with a photo from Wikimedia Commons, all under
free licences. The tiles carry no caption — at roughly 50px a credit line is unreadable
clutter — so **this file is the attribution** for the CC BY and CC BY-SA images below,
which require it. Keep it alongside the app if you publish or share it.

These are a starting point, not a fixture. Anyone with the trip code can open a photo
and remove it to free the tile, then add their own. Removing a curated photo does not
destroy it — the original stays in `itinerary.json` and can be put back.

**63 photos** across four days.

## A note on subject matter

Deliberately buildings, food, streets and skyline rather than sculpture. US freedom of
panorama covers architecture but not public sculpture, which makes photographs of Cloud
Gate, the Picasso and Crown Fountain murkier to redistribute — so those stops are
represented by their surroundings instead.
